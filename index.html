Soulcraft108 Pin Planner

Soulcraft108 Pin Planner is a private tool used only by the shop owner. When a product is published in the shop, it creates a few Pinterest Pins for that product (images made from the product's own pages, with a link back to its listing) and posts them, spaced out over several days, to boards on the shop's own Pinterest account. It can also read the performance of those Pins (impressions, saves and clicks) so the owner can see what is working.

It has no other users and is not offered to the public. It never posts to anyone else's account and does not read other people's Pins, boards, messages or followers.
